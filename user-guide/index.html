1 Getting started

When you open Yarn Stasher, you can sign in to save your yarn to the cloud and manage your stash on more than one device, or you can jump in as a guest. Guest mode keeps your yarn on this device until you decide to create an account and transfer your stash.

  1. 1 Tap Get Started to sign in or create an account. You can use an email address, Google, or Facebook. Prefer to look around first? You can browse as a guest from the next screen.
  1. 1 Already have an account? Enter your email and password, then tap Sign In.
  2. 2 New here? Tap Create New Account to make an account using your email and a password. Yarn Stasher will send you a verification email to make sure it’s you.
  3. 3 Or sign in with Google or Facebook, or tap Browse as Guest to skip the account for now.

Verify your email for 10 free scans

If you signed up with an email and password, open the verification email we sent and tap the link inside. Then come back to the app, open ☰ → Settings, and confirm at the top of the page. You’ll get 10 free Smart Scan credits as a thank-you. Didn’t get the email? You can resend it from the same place.

Starting as a guest? When you sign in later, Yarn Stasher will offer to move your guest stash and any scan credits into your account. Your yarn stays on the device until you say yes, so nothing is lost if you skip it. Guests can also link to Ravelry.

2 Connect to Ravelry

If you use Ravelry, you can connect it to Yarn Stasher. That brings your existing Ravelry stash into the app, and anything you add in Yarn Stasher shows up on Ravelry, ready to use in your projects. It works whether you have a Yarn Stasher account or are browsing as a guest. You’ll need an internet connection.

Link your Ravelry account

  1. 1 Open ☰, then tap Settings.
  2. 2 On the Ravelry Integration card, tap Link Ravelry Account.
  3. 3 Your browser opens Ravelry. Sign in there and allow Yarn Stasher. Stay on that page until it sends you back to the app.
  4. 4 Back in Settings, the card now shows your Ravelry username. Tap Sync Stash Now to bring your yarn over.

If the browser doesn’t bring you back to Yarn Stasher, just open Settings and tap Link Ravelry Account again.

Once you’re linked

  1. 1 Re-auth signs you in to Ravelry again. Every so often Ravelry asks for a fresh sign-in to keep your account secure. If Yarn Stasher tells you the connection needs refreshing, tap this and sign in. Your stash isn’t affected.
  2. 2 Ravelry opens your Ravelry profile in your browser.
  3. 3 Unlink Ravelry Account disconnects the two. Your yarn stays on this device.

Want to match a single yarn to its official Ravelry page? That’s a separate step, covered in Link to database yarns.

3 Your stash

This is home base. After you sign in (or continue as a guest), you’ll land here every time you open the app.

  1. 1 Tap Filters to search your stash or narrow it down.
  2. 2 Tap the sort button (it shows the current sorting category, here Brand) to choose how your yarn is ordered. The arrow next to it flips between A–Z and Z–A.
  3. 3 Tap the grid/list icon to switch between big photos and a compact list.
  4. 4 Tap ☰ to open the menu.
  5. 5 Tap the cloud icon to sync your stash right now. You’ll see it when you’re signed in or have Ravelry linked.
  6. 6 Tap + Add Yarn to add a new yarn.
  7. 7 Your yarn count. This stash has 215 yarns. When a filter is on, it shows how many match, like “12 of 215 yarns”.

A few more things you might notice on this screen:

  • If your stash is empty, Yarn Stasher will invite you to add your first yarn.
  • Pull down on the screen to sync whenever you’re online.
  • If you lose your internet connection, a notice appears at the top. You can keep browsing and editing; see Offline mode.

The menu

  1. 1 Sign Out is at the top of the menu. If you’re browsing as a guest, you’ll see Sign In here instead.
  2. 2 Home takes you back to your stash. About shows the app version and helpful links. Settings is where you’ll find sync, Ravelry, theme, and units.
  3. 3 Smart Scan Credits shows how many scans you have left. Tap Buy to purchase more.

Going back

The Back button (or the back arrow at the top of the screen on iPhone) undoes one thing at a time: first it leaves selection mode, then it closes the Filters panel, then it clears any filters you had on.

4 Browse & organize

List view

Grid view is great for browsing by eye. List view fits more yarn on the screen and shows more detail for each one.

  1. 1 Tap the grid/list icon to switch back to photos.
  2. 2 Tap any yarn to open it and see or change its details.

Sort

  1. 1 Tap the sort button. It shows the current sorting category, here Brand.
  2. 2 Tap a category to sort by: Name, Colorway, Brand, Total Length, Weight, or Date Updated. Your stash re-sorts right away.
  3. 3 Changed your mind? Tap Close to leave things as they are.

Filters & search

Looking for something specific, like all your worsted-weight wool or that one blue skein? Filters show only the yarn that matches.

  1. 1 Tap Filters to open or close the panel. The button is highlighted while the panel is open.
  2. 2 Type in Search your stash… to find yarn by brand, name, colorway, color, dye lot, or anything in your notes.
  3. 3 Tap the small rounded buttons to filter by group, brand, fiber, weight, or color. Tap one again to turn it off. You can combine as many as you like.

When you pick more than one fiber, OR shows yarn containing any of them, and AND shows only yarn containing all of them. Scroll down in the panel to filter by handspun yarn, or by length and weight.

5 Add, edit & delete yarn

Tap + Add Yarn on your stash to start a new entry. The quickest way to fill it in is to take a photo of the label and let Smart Scan do the typing, but you can always enter everything yourself.

  1. 1 Tap the camera box to take a photo or pick one from your gallery. You can add up to four photos of a single yarn. Tap the trash can icon on a photo to remove it.
  2. 2 This is the Smart Scan button. It reads “Add photos first” until you’ve added a photo. Once you have, you can use Smart Scan as long as you’re connected to the internet. See the next section for details.
  3. 3 Tap Add Yarn to save your new yarn to your stash.
  4. 4 Tap the back arrow to leave without saving. If you have unsaved changes, you’ll be asked whether you want to stay on the page or discard them.

Edit a yarn

Tap any yarn in your stash to open it. Change whatever you like, then tap Save Changes.

Possible duplicates

If a new yarn looks like one you might already have, Yarn Stasher will flag it as a possible duplicate and show you the yarn it found. You can tap Cancel to go back to your entry, Add skeins to add these skeins to the yarn you already have, or Save new to keep it as a separate entry.

Same yarn, another colorway

Bought the same yarn in a different colorway? Open the one you already have and tap Add Another Colorway under Basic Info. Yarn Stasher copies the brand, name, yarn weight, fiber content, and other details into a new entry for you. Photos, colorway, and amount start out empty, ready for you to fill in.

Delete a yarn

Open the yarn and tap the trash can icon at the top of the screen. To delete several at once, press and hold a yarn in your stash, then tap Delete (see Groups & selecting several yarns).

6 Photos & Smart Scan

Smart Scan reads the photos of your yarn label and fills in your stash entry for you. No typing needed.

For the best results: include photos where the yarn label is clearly visible, well lit, and in focus. The more of the label Smart Scan can see (brand, colorway, weight, fiber content, yardage), the more it can fill in. If the label wraps around the skein, take a photo of each side.
  1. 1 Add your photos (up to four), then tap Smart Scan. It fills in the brand, name, colorway, yarn weight, fiber content, and yardage, based on what it could read from the yarn label.
  2. 2 Give the details a quick look. Labels can be tricky, and a scan can occasionally misread one. Fix anything that’s off, then tap Add Yarn (or Save Changes if you’re editing).
  • One scan uses one credit, no matter how many photos you attached. The button shows how many credits you have left.
  • Smart Scan needs an internet connection.
  • Curious how it read your label? Tap View analysis details after a scan.

7 Yarn details

Every yarn in your stash can hold as much or as little detail as you like. Here’s what each part of the page is for.

  1. 1 Basic Info covers the brand, name, colorway, yarn weight, and closest color. Closest color is a simple color family (like blue or green) that makes your yarn easy to find with filters.
  2. 2 Measurements covers how many skeins you have, plus the yardage (or meterage) and weight of each skein. Yarn Stasher adds up the totals for you as you type.

Prefer yards to meters, or ounces to grams? You can change your units in Settings.

Fiber content

Add each fiber and its percentage, for example 80% Merino and 20% Nylon. As you type, Yarn Stasher suggests fibers you’ve used before.

Groups

Groups are your own way of sorting your stash into sub-categories: a project you’re planning, the bin a yarn lives in, a season, anything you like. Add a yarn to a group here, and that group shows up as a button in Filters.

Extra details

There’s also room for the dye lot, needle size, gauge, and your own notes, and you can mark a yarn as hand spun.

Skein Count Calculator

Yarn Stasher includes a calculator to help you keep track of partial skeins. Use it to update a yarn based on how much you used, or how much you have left. To open it, scroll to Measurements and tap Skein Count Calculator.

  1. 1 Subtract is for yarn you’ve used. Enter the skeins, length, or weight you knit or crocheted up.
  2. 2 Add is for when you’ve bought more of the same yarn.
  3. 3 Remaining is for when you know what’s left. Weigh your leftover yarn, enter the amount, and Yarn Stasher works out the new skein count.

8 Groups & selecting several yarns

You can create groups to organize your stash into sub-categories. The fastest way to fill a group is to select several yarns at once.

  1. 1 Press and hold a yarn until checkboxes appear. Then tap any other yarns you want to include.
  2. 2 Choose what to do with them: Assign Group adds them to a group, Remove Group takes them out of one, and Delete removes them from your stash. Yarn Stasher always asks before deleting.
  3. 3 Tap Cancel when you’re done.

9 Smart Scan credits

Each Smart Scan uses one credit. You get some free to start, and more are available to purchase in the app to make stashing faster. Everything else in Yarn Stasher is free: adding photos to an entry or typing in a yarn’s details yourself never uses credits.

Free credits

  • When you first install the app, you get 3 free credits on that device, whether you sign in or browse as a guest.
  • When you create an account and verify your email, you get 10 more. See Getting started. If you sign up with Google, your email is already verified, so they’re added right away.
  • If you started as a guest and sign in later, any credits you have left can move into your account along with your stash.

Buying more

  1. 1 Open ☰ and tap Buy. You can buy credits in packs of 10, 50, 200, and 500. Tap a pack to purchase it. If you’re browsing as a guest, you may be asked to sign in first.
  2. 2 Got a new phone or reinstalled the app? Tap Restore Purchases to bring back credits you’ve already bought.

Buying credits requires an internet connection.

10 Sync & backup

Your stash is always saved on your device, so it’s there even without internet. Where else it’s kept depends on how you use the app:

  • Signed in to Yarn Stasher: your stash is backed up to Yarn Stasher’s cloud and can be accessed through your account on any device.
  • Linked to Ravelry: your stash syncs with your Ravelry stash instead, and Ravelry becomes its home. This is true whether you’re signed in or a guest.
  • Guest, without Ravelry: your stash lives only on this device. Create an account or link Ravelry to keep it safe if anything happens to your phone.

Yarn Stasher syncs on its own as you go. To sync right now, tap the cloud icon on your stash, pull down on the stash screen, or open Settings and tap Sync Stash Now.

11 Link to database yarns

Just like in Ravelry, you can link each yarn in your stash to its page in Ravelry’s yarn database, or keep it as a “personal yarn” with your own details. Linking fills in any details you’re missing, and lets Ravelry recognize exactly which yarn you have. You’ll need to connect your Ravelry account first.

  1. 1 Tap Link to Database Yarn. Yarn Stasher looks through Ravelry’s database for yarns that match yours.
  2. 2 Pick the right match from the list. Yarn Stasher links your yarn and fills in anything that was missing.
  3. 3 Don’t see your yarn? Skip it. Your entry stays a personal yarn with the details you entered.

Once a yarn is linked, its brand, name, yarn weight, and fiber content come straight from Ravelry, so those can’t be edited. On a linked yarn you can tap View on Ravelry to see its Ravelry page, or Convert to Personal Yarn to unlink it and edit those details yourself again.

When you sync with Ravelry, Yarn Stasher may also offer to find matches for yarns you haven’t linked yet. It works the same way: pick a match, or skip.

12 Settings

Open ☰, then tap Settings. Sync and Ravelry are at the top; they’re covered in Sync & backup and Connect to Ravelry.

  1. 1 Theme changes how the app looks. Flip the Dark mode switch, or choose between your phone’s own setting, light, and dark.
  1. 1 Units lets you choose meters or yards, and grams or ounces. Your whole stash switches over, so there’s nothing to convert yourself.
  2. 2 Help & Legal has this guide, our Privacy Policy and Terms, and Contact Support if you’d like to get in touch. If you’re signed in, your account options are just below.

13 Account

You can sign in to the same Yarn Stasher account in more than one way. For example, add Google to an account you created with an email and password, and either one will get you in.

  1. 1 Open Settings and tap Linked Accounts. You’ll see each way to sign in: email and password, Google, and Facebook. Tap + Link next to one to add it to your account.

Forgot your password?

Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen and we’ll email you a link to reset it.

Sign out

Open ☰ and tap Sign Out at the top of the menu.

Delete your account

Open Settings, go to your account options, and tap Delete Account. You’ll be asked to sign in once more to confirm it’s really you. Deleting your account is permanent and can’t be undone. See Account Deletion for exactly what is removed.

14 Offline mode

No signal at the yarn shop? No problem. Your stash is saved on your device, so you can still browse it, search it, and make changes.

While you’re offline, you’ll see a notice at the top of the screen. A few things have to wait until you’re connected again: syncing, Smart Scan, buying credits, and most changes to your account.

If you have an account or Ravelry linked, the changes you made offline will sync once you’re back online.

15 Troubleshooting

Smart Scan won’t run
Smart Scan needs three things: at least one photo on the yarn, an internet connection, and at least one credit. If the button says “Add photos first”, add a photo of the label. If you’re out of credits, you can buy more from the menu, or verify your email to claim your 10 free credits if you haven’t already.
My stash won’t sync
First, check that you’re connected to the internet. Then open Settings, where the sync card will tell you what went wrong. If you sync with Ravelry, tap Re-auth on the Ravelry card to sign in again. That fixes most Ravelry sync problems.
Ravelry won’t link
Open Settings and tap Link Ravelry Account. Sign in to Ravelry in the browser, allow Yarn Stasher, and wait for it to send you back to the app. If it doesn’t come back, start again from the same button. See Connect to Ravelry.
My purchased credits are missing
Open ☰ → Buy and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re online and signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought them with.
My guest stash is missing after I signed in
If you skipped the transfer when you signed in, your guest yarn is still on the device you were using. Email us at support@yarnstasher.com and we’ll help you get it back.
Yarns aren’t showing up in my stash
Try clearing any filters. Check the yarn count at the top of your stash: if it says something like “0 of 215 yarns”, a filter is hiding them. Open Filters and turn off the highlighted buttons, or clear the search box.
Something else?
We’re happy to help. Email support@yarnstasher.com.

17 Glossary

Stash
All the yarn you’ve added to Yarn Stasher.
Guest mode
Using the app without a Yarn Stasher account. Your stash is kept on your device.
Smart Scan
Reads the photos of your yarn label and fills in the details for you.
Scan credits
What Smart Scan runs on. One scan uses one credit.
Groups
Your own sub-categories for your stash, like a project, a storage bin, or a season.
Closest color
A simple color family (like blue or green) used for filtering, alongside the yarn’s own colorway name.
Cloud sync
Yarn Stasher’s online backup, for signed-in accounts that aren’t linked to Ravelry.
Ravelry link
The connection between Yarn Stasher and your Ravelry account, which keeps the two stashes matched.
Database yarn
A yarn in your stash that’s linked to its page in Ravelry’s yarn database.
Personal yarn
A yarn that isn’t linked to Ravelry’s database, so every detail is yours to edit.
