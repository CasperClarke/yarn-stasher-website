What Yarn Stasher does

Scan it, don’t type it

Take up to four photos of a label and tap Smart Scan. Brand, name, colorway, weight, fiber, and yardage are filled in for you.

Find it anywhere

In the yarn shop wondering if you already own that colorway? Search and filter your stash. It’s saved on your phone, so it works with no signal.

Works with Ravelry

Link your Ravelry account to bring in the stash you already have there. Yarn you add in the app shows up on Ravelry too.

Free to organize

Organizing, searching, syncing, and Ravelry are free, with no subscription. Smart Scan uses credits: you get free scans to start, and packs are there if you want more.

Made by people who love yarn

Do you have a large collection of yarn? We know the challenge of keeping track of it all.

Yarn Stasher helps you inventory and catalog your entire stash without typing. Take a picture of your yarn labels, and Smart Scan reads the brand, colorway, weight, fiber content, and yardage for you. Let the app do the bookkeeping while you focus on what you love: making beautiful things.

What’s new & what’s next

New in version 1.14

A big round of fixes, many from your emails:

  • Smarter search: “blue merino” finds yarn that’s both
  • Accented names (like Café) sort A–Z
  • “Hats, Gifts” makes two groups, and they stay that way after Ravelry sync
  • If one photo can’t be read, the rest are still added
  • Guest stash keeps groups and photos when you create an account
  • Clearer error messages and more reliable account deletion

Coming soon

  • Ply count. An optional ply count on each yarn, plus a filter to search your stash by ply.

Have an idea? Tell us. Many of the fixes in 1.14 started as an email.
