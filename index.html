What Yarn Stasher does

Scan it, don’t type it

Take up to four photos of a label and tap Smart Scan. Brand, name, colorway, weight, fiber, and yardage are filled in for you.

Find it anywhere

In the yarn shop wondering if you already own that colorway? Search and filter your stash. It’s saved on your phone, so it works with no signal.

Works with Ravelry

Link your Ravelry account to bring in the stash you already have there. Yarn you add in the app shows up on Ravelry too.

Free to organize

Organizing, searching, syncing, and Ravelry are free, with no subscription. Smart Scan uses credits: you get free scans to start, and packs are there if you want more.

Our Passion for Yarn

Do you have a large collection of yarn?

We know the challenge of keeping track of it all. Yarn Stasher helps you inventory and catalog your entire stash without typing any information. Simply take a picture of your yarn labels, and Yarn Stasher's AI automatically extracts all the details—brand, color, weight, fiber content, and yardage. Let technology do the work while you focus on what you love: creating beautiful projects.
